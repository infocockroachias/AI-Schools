LVL: CLASS 6 · LEVEL 1 — NOTICE | 20 PAPER MISSIONS · 5 CHAPTERS · NO SCREEN NEEDED

--- CH 1: Machines That Seem Smart  [ch1.typ]  missions T6-01 – T6-05
    Q: When is a machine actually learning — and when is it only following steps?
    LINK: Links: Maths — patterns · English — exact instructions
    WORDS: algorithm, automation, artificial intelligence, training examples, predict
    SUMMARY: Every detective starts by learning to tell two suspects apart. In this chapter you will meet *rule-followers* — machines that run exact steps — and *learners* — machines that improve from examples. By the last mission you will have played a human robot, taught an alien using only flashcards, and judged a fair humans-vs-machines contest.

--- CH 2: Data: The Food of AI  [ch2.typ]  missions T6-06 – T6-09
    Q: What do machines learn from — and what happens when the food is bad?
    LINK: Links: Maths — tables & bar graphs · Science — observation
    WORDS: data, pixel, tally, garbage in, garbage out, bar chart
    SUMMARY: Machines cannot learn on empty stomachs — their food is *data*. You will hunt the four kinds of data around your classroom, tidy them into tables, turn a table into a chart, and then discover what happens when a learner is fed bad examples. Garbage in, garbage out — and only a detective can spot why.

--- CH 3: Patterns & Decisions  [ch3.typ]  missions T6-10 – T6-13
    Q: How does a machine decide what to do next?
    LINK: Links: Maths — number patterns · English — clear questions
    WORDS: pattern, prediction, confidence, if-then, question tree
    SUMMARY: Patterns are the clues learners chase: find one, predict what comes next, and then say *how sure* you are. You will hunt number and letter patterns, play Guess My Rule with a hidden rule-keeper, trace an if-then decision machine, and debate answers whose confidence is higher than their accuracy.

--- CH 4: Be a Smart Digital Citizen  [ch4.typ]  missions T6-14 – T6-17
    Q: How do I enjoy the online world — and stay safe, kind and honest?
    LINK: Links: Social Science — community life · English — checking facts
    WORDS: privacy, personal information, passphrase, verify, digital footprint
    SUMMARY: Smart detectives stay safe, kind and honest — online as everywhere. You will sort personal information with a privacy traffic light, cook unguessable practice passphrases, run the three-check Real-or-Fake detector on suspect cards, and follow one drawing's digital footprint as it travels the internet.

--- CH 5: My Neighbourhood AI Investigation  [ch5.typ]  missions T6-18 – T6-20
    Q: What can I discover about AI near me — using my own data?
    LINK: Links: Social Science — community survey · Maths — data & charts
    WORDS: survey, tally, bar chart, limit, evidence
    SUMMARY: Your biggest case yet: a *real survey* of five grown-ups about the smart machines around them. You will plan and run the survey, organise and chart the data you collect, design a helper machine of your own — and then close the case by answering the big question from page one, with evidence.
