A neural network is layers of weighted votes
T10-02 makes you the neurons. In the drawing, every line is a weight, every circle is one vote — intelligence from arithmetic, not magic.
One neuron's whole job: multiply each incoming value by its weight, add them up, and pass the total through a simple squashing rule. Bold lines = strong weights — training is just the slow search for better numbers in those lines.
Layers build meaning step by step: early layers answer tiny questions (edges, sounds, word pairs); deeper layers combine those into bigger ones. Each layer only ever votes with the layer before it.
MYTH CHECK  "Neural networks work like the brain." They are inspired by neurons the way a paper plane is inspired by a bird — the real brain has no training phase, no weights table and no output layer. Say "layers of weighted votes", not "it thinks".
