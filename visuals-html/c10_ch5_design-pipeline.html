The Responsible AI Design Brief — seven stages, two gates
T10-19 walks your capstone through the full pipeline. The two gates are where most real projects should die — and where the honest ones admit it early.
STAGE 1

Scope

Sharp problem statement: who, what, where, why now.

PROBLEM SHEET
STAGE 2

Data

Model

Evaluate

EVIDENCE TABLE
STAGE 5

Deploy

Who uses it, where it fails, what humans stay in the loop.

USE POLICY
STAGE 6

Ethics review

Four principles per group; transparency and accountability named.

ETHICS NOTE
STAGE 7

Pitch

Board of judges: claim, evidence, limits, and your own doubts.

PITCH CARD
GATE A — after Stage 2: "Would the people in this data consent to this use — and is any group missing?" If the answer is no, Stage 3 must not start. Redesign the data, not the pitch.
GATE B — after Stage 6: "Would we be proud to explain every failure to the people it affects?" If any principle fails, the brief returns to Stage 2 — with the reason written down.
